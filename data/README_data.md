# Data availability and scope

This folder describes the data scope for the manuscript entitled:

"Climatic-baseline choice and temporal mismatch in species distribution models: effects on habitat suitability and transferability for Hemiscorpius acanthocercus (Scorpiones: Hemiscorpiidae) in Iran"

The occurrence dataset used in this study consists of environmentally filtered presence records prepared for MaxEnt calibration at the harmonized environmental grid level.

The raw occurrence database, specimen-level coordinates, field notes, and unpublished dissertation datasets are not publicly redistributed in this repository because they are part of an ongoing PhD dissertation and planned follow-up analyses.

Processed occurrence data may be made available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request, subject to author approval and data-use conditions.

Raw environmental raster datasets are not redistributed because they are publicly available from their original providers, including TerraClimate, WorldClim/CMIP6, SoilGrids, and Copernicus DEM GLO-30.

This repository provides supporting scripts and workflow documentation for the analyses reported in the manuscript, while protecting unpublished data that are not necessary to publicly redistribute at this stage.
