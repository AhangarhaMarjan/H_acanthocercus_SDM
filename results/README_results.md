# Results availability and scope

This folder describes the scope of derived results associated with the manuscript entitled:

"Climatic-baseline choice and temporal mismatch in species distribution models: effects on habitat suitability and transferability for Hemiscorpius acanthocercus (Scorpiones: Hemiscorpiidae) in Iran"

The numerical results reported in the manuscript include MaxEnt tuning summaries, final model settings, habitat-area estimates, four-class habitat dynamics, eight-class trajectory summaries, spatial agreement metrics, centroid displacement summaries, and MESS/MoD summaries.

At this stage, detailed derived result tables are not redistributed in this repository because the manuscript is under preparation/submission and the broader dissertation dataset is still ongoing.

Summary results reported in the manuscript are available in the manuscript tables and figures. Additional derived tables may be provided by the corresponding author upon reasonable request, subject to author approval.

Large raster outputs, intermediate GIS layers, ArcGIS project files, and unpublished dissertation outputs are not publicly redistributed in this repository.
